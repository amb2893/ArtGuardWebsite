#  ArtGuard

**ArtGuard** is a community-driven platform for evaluating how websites, platforms, and AI services treat and protect artists.

Our goal is to bring **transparency, accountability, and visibility** to online platforms — especially in an era where artwork is frequently scraped, reused, or monetized without consent.

---

##  Why ArtGuard Exists

Artists deserve to know:
- Which websites respect copyright and consent
- Which platforms allow opt-outs or ethical data use
- Which services are repeatedly reported for art misuse

ArtGuard enables users to **report**, **discuss**, and **rate** websites based on how they protect (or fail to protect) artists.

---

## Features

-  **Website Tracking**
  - Maintain a database of websites and platforms
  - Track community report counts per site

-  **Community Ratings**
  - Users can upvote or downvote websites
  - One rating per user per website (to prevent vote spam)

-  **Discussion Forum**
  - Create discussion threads
  - Comment on posts
  - Share research, experiences, and tools (e.g., Nightshade, Glaze)

-  **Articles & Resources**
  - Publish educational articles about copyright, AI, and artist protection

-  **User Accounts**
  - Account-based posting and voting
  - Admin-ready role structure (future expansion)
